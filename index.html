Cybersecurity: Protecting the Digital World

Learn how to protect yourself online.

What Is Cybersecurity?

Cybersecurity is the practice of protecting computers, networks, and personal information from online threats. It helps keep our digital world safe.

Common Cyber Threats

  • Phishing emails and fake websites
  • Viruses and harmful software
  • Password theft
  • Data breaches

How to Stay Safe Online

  • Use strong passwords.
  • Enable two-factor authentication.
  • Keep your computer and apps updated.
  • Avoid suspicious links.
  • Never share private information with strangers.
